Privacy Policy — Clipper

Privacy Policy — Clipper Last updated: August 25, 2026 Who we are Clipper (“the App”) is a personal tool operated by Omar Abdelaziz. Contact: omar.moh.abdelaziz@gmail.com. What the App does Clipper runs on my own computer. It downloads public YouTube video data I choose, creates short vertical clips, and can upload drafts to platforms I connect (YouTube and/or TikTok) using their official APIs. Information collected The App may process: Account / OAuth data from Google (YouTube) and TikTok when I connect an account (for example access and refresh tokens, channel or open ID, display name). Content I provide, such as YouTube URLs, titles, descriptions, and generated video/caption files stored on my machine. Technical logs on my machine for debugging (errors, job status). Tokens and secrets are not intended to be logged. The App does not operate a public multi-user service and does not sell personal data. How information is used To authenticate with YouTube / TikTok and upload or draft content to accounts I authorize To run the clipping pipeline (analyze, cut, caption, render, review) To store encrypted credentials locally so I do not have to reconnect every time Storage and security Data and media files are stored on my local device (and any backup location I choose). OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest when a local encryption key is configured. I am responsible for securing my computer, .env secrets, and backups. Sharing I do not sell or rent personal data. Data may be sent only to: Google / YouTube and TikTok as needed for OAuth and publishing, under their terms and privacy policies OpenAI (or similar) if I enable transcription/captions, for audio I submit to that API Third-party services Use of Google, TikTok, OpenAI, and similar providers is also governed by those providers’ policies. Retention I keep local job files and account tokens until I delete them or uninstall the App. I may revoke platform access anytime in Google / TikTok account settings. Your rights Because this is a personal tool for my own use, “users” are limited to me (and any test accounts I add). Platform account holders can revoke OAuth access in their Google or TikTok settings. Changes I may update this policy by changing this page and updating the “Last updated” date.
